About Psychic Review Directory

We are an independent review platform dedicated to helping you find trusted, verified psychic readers and services. Since 2021, our editorial team has tested and reviewed hundreds of platforms, readers, and services across every major category – tarot, astrology, mediumship, energy healing, love readings, and more.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: bring clarity to an industry that often lacks transparency. Every review on our platform is based on real sessions, verified client feedback, and rigorous editorial standards. We accept zero sponsorship from the platforms we review.

How We Review

Each platform and reader in our directory goes through a multi-step evaluation process:

  • Anonymous test readings – our reviewers book sessions as regular clients
  • Client feedback aggregation – we collect and verify reviews from real users
  • Pricing transparency check – we document all costs, hidden fees, and refund policies
  • Platform safety audit – privacy policies, data handling, and payment security
  • Reader verification – background checks on credentials and claimed specialties

Editorial Independence

Psychic Review Directory is 100% independently funded. We may earn commissions through affiliate links, but this never influences our ratings or recommendations. Our reviews are written first, and affiliate relationships are added later – never the other way around.
